Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start recording a ride?

Open Longboard Speedometer, tap the button to start a ride, and allow location access when prompted.

Why does the app need my location?

Location access is used to calculate your speed, distance, elevation, and route while recording a ride.

Where are my rides stored?

Your rides are stored locally on your device. Longboard Speedometer does not require an account.

How do I delete a ride?

Open the saved ride and select the delete option.
