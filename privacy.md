# Privacy Policy for Longboard Speedometer

**Last updated: July 8, 2026**

Longboard Speedometer is designed with privacy in mind. Your ride data belongs to you.

Your location is used only while the app is recording a ride or displaying live ride information.

## Information We Collect

Longboard Speedometer does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to our servers.

The app may access location only to calculate speed, distance, route, elevation, and ride statistics.

## How Your Data Is Stored

Ride information—including routes, speed, distance, elevation, weather, notes, and ride names—is stored locally on your device. Longboard Speedometer does not upload or back up ride data to a server.

## Sharing

If you choose to share a ride, the app generates a shareable image on your device. No ride data is sent to the developer unless you explicitly choose to share it.

## Feedback

If you choose Settings → Feedback, your device’s email app opens with a pre-addressed email. Any information you send is voluntary and is used only to respond to your feedback or improve the app.

## Third-Party Services

Longboard Speedometer does not include third-party software that collects usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or personal information from users.

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, cloud-storage services, or user accounts.

If this changes in a future release, this policy will be updated.

## Children’s Privacy

Longboard Speedometer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Changes will be reflected here with a revised “Last updated” date.
